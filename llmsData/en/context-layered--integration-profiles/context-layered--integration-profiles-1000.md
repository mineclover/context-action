Integration Profiles

Integration Profiles Integration profiles are versioned Context-Action conventions for an external domain. They do not add that domain's types or business rules to @context-action/core; they supply a catalog of lifecycle, ownership, compatibility, and evidence requirements. Lifecycle draft → registered → verified → supported → deprecated - draft: proposal only; not a consumer contract

• **draft**: proposal only; not a consumer contract.
• **registered**: an action/state ownership manifest and consumer are named.
